Data gaps

We only chart data that is complete enough to compare. This page shows what is missing for each condition, so you can see what the charts leave out.

Data updated .

Trial data: how many trials have each piece of information

Each cell is the share of that condition's trials that have the information. A trial missing it is left out of the chart that needs it.

Missing race or ethnicity does not necessarily mean a group was left out of a trial. The information may not have been collected or reported.

Patient data: what the comparison is waiting for

The patient comparison needs both a trial share and a patient share for the same group. We only show it when both are complete.

Why some groups have no match

Trials record race and ethnicity separately, but our patient source does not always. Some patient groups include Hispanic patients and some exclude them, so a few comparisons are approximate. "More than one race" has no matching patient group.
